# Phase 1 – Brainstorming & Ideation

## Project Title
**PocketSmart AI: Your Smart Budget & Recommendation Assistant**

## Problem Statement
Managing budgets for home interiors, parties, and jewelry shopping can be difficult because users must compare many products, services, platforms, and price ranges.

## Proposed Idea
PocketSmart AI is a GenAI-powered assistant that accepts a user's budget and preferences and produces personalized recommendations.

## Main Modules
1. Home Interior Planner
2. Party Budget Planner
3. Jewelry Budget Planner

## Target Users
People who want practical, budget-aware suggestions for lifestyle planning and shopping.

## Expected Benefits
- Budget-conscious planning
- Personalized recommendations
- One interface for multiple planning categories
- Optional image-based jewelry analysis
- Recommendation history for reuse

## AI Concept
The project uses Gemini as the recommendation/analysis layer. The project document specifies text, numerical budget information, and optional images as inputs.
